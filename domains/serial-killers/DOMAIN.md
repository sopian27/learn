# Serial Killer Psychology — Domain Pack

## Domain Name

Serial Killer Psychology (Psikologi Serial Killer)

## Scope

The academic forensic-psychology and criminology specialization of studying serial murderers as individual case subjects: what defines "serial murder" and how it's classified (FBI definition vs. mass/spree murder), the history of the classification itself, psychopathy and personality-disorder frameworks as applied to offenders (Hare's PCL-R and its misuse), the neuroscience of psychopathy (documented brain-imaging research), developmental/trauma etiology (violentization theory and comparable peer-reviewed frameworks — correlational, not deterministic), compulsive behavior patterns and victim-selection research, manipulation tactics, offender-profiling methodology and its documented accuracy limitations, warning-sign literature and why retrospective pattern-matching isn't predictive, and pop-culture myth debunking (the "criminal mastermind" myth specifically).

Does **NOT** duplicate already-registered domains or the sibling course/domain this was drafted alongside:
- `domains/psychology/DOMAIN.md` — supplies the general scientific base (personality theory, DSM-5-TR classification broadly, biopsychosocial model). This domain does not re-teach general psychopathology or personality theory from scratch; it applies psychopathy/antisocial-personality-disorder frameworks specifically and deeply to the serial-murder offender population as case subjects. General psychopathology descriptive content stays in `domains/psychology/DOMAIN.md` Module 13.
- The sibling course "Kasus Kriminal Terkenal Dunia" (expected at `courses/famous-criminal-cases/roadmap.md`, not yet on disk as of this domain's registration) — covers broad true-crime/forensic-science-methodology/trial-process/wrongful-convictions across all crime types. This domain does NOT cover general forensic science (DNA, ballistics, toxicology), courtroom/trial procedure, or non-serial-murder case journalism. If a domain pack is registered for that sibling course later, cross-reference it here and keep the boundary: that domain owns case breadth and legal/forensic process; this domain owns serial-murder-specific psychological/behavioral/neuroscience depth.

Explicit framing constraint: this domain is academic criminology/forensic psychology, never true-crime sensationalism, glorification, or "anti-hero"/genius-criminal narrative framing. Every case study must be treated as a documented psychological subject, not entertainment.

## Trusted Sources / Research Priority

1. **Robert Hare** — *Without Conscience* and the official PCL-R manual/technical literature, for what the checklist actually measures — paired always with peer-reviewed critiques of its overuse/misuse (e.g. Skeem & Cooke 2010, DeMatteo et al.) so the tool is never taught as infallible.
2. **FBI Behavioral Science Unit primary literature** — Ressler, Burgess & Douglas's *Sexual Homicide: Patterns and Motives* and the *Crime Classification Manual* for the organized/disorganized typology as originally proposed — paired always with the FBI's own acknowledgment that the typology is oversimplified plus independent academic critiques (Snook et al. 2007 "Taking Stock of Criminal Profiling," Canter's work questioning the typology's empirical basis) so profiling is taught with its real, documented accuracy limitations, never as forensic science with courtroom-grade validity.
3. **Kent Kiehl** — *The Psychopath Whisperer* and peer-reviewed neuroimaging papers (paralimbic system dysfunction findings) for the neuroscience module — always caveated with sample-bias limitations (incarcerated/adjudicated populations, correlation vs. causation, brain differences as one factor among many, not deterministic proof).
4. **Lonnie Athens** — *Violent Criminal Acts and Actors* (violentization/violentization theory) and comparable peer-reviewed developmental-criminology frameworks for trauma/developmental factors — taught as correlational and non-deterministic (most people with similar developmental histories do not become violent offenders).
5. **Court records, trial transcripts, and reputable long-form journalism/academic case studies** for individual cases (Bundy, Rader, Dahmer) — never tabloid speculation, never unverified pop-true-crime claims. When a claim can't be traced to a court record, confession transcript, or peer-reviewed/reputable journalistic source, it doesn't go in a lesson.
6. **Serial Killer Information Center** aggregate data (e.g. IQ distribution across documented offenders) — used only for the Myths module's statistical mythbusting, cross-checked against academic sources per `standards/UNIVERSAL_STANDARDS.md`'s Vetting a Source bar.

**House rule specific to this domain:** every framework or tool introduced (PCL-R, organized/disorganized typology, brain-imaging findings, warning-sign checklists) must be taught with its documented accuracy/reliability limitations in the same lesson it's introduced — never as a settled, infallible predictive instrument. This mirrors `domains/psychology/DOMAIN.md`'s replication-crisis house rule, applied here to forensic-psychology tools instead of classic experiments.

## Applied Practice Definition

Applying a named forensic-psychology framework to a documented case, using only primary/documented sources (court records, confession transcripts, peer-reviewed case literature, reputable journalism) — never speculative armchair diagnosis of living private individuals, and never framed for entertainment. Examples: scoring a documented offender's publicly-recorded behavior against PCL-R trait categories (with explicit caveats about the limits of doing this without a clinical interview), mapping a case's developmental record onto violentization stages, critiquing a real offender-profiling report's methodology, or writing a structured case analysis citing only documented sources.

## Project Tiers

- **Mini Project** (per module): a single-framework application to one documented case element — e.g. mapping one case's cooling-off pattern to compulsive-behavior research, or critiquing one myth against the Serial Killer Information Center's aggregate data.
- **Intermediate Project** (per case-study module): a structured psychological profile of one documented case (Bundy or Rader) combining personality framework + developmental factors + manipulation-tactic analysis, citing only primary/documented sources.
- **Capstone / Portfolio Project** (end of course): a full structured case-analysis report applying the course's integrated framework (classification → personality/neuroscience → developmental factors → behavioral pattern → profiling-methodology critique → myth-check) to one well-documented case, with a explicit sourcing appendix (every claim traceable to a court record, confession transcript, or peer-reviewed/reputable source), presentable and saved to `portofolio/`.

## Review Style

On top of the Universal Review Rubric (Strengths / Weaknesses / Actionable Improvements / Score 0–100), every review additionally checks:

- **Source Rigor** — is every factual claim traceable to a court record, confession transcript, peer-reviewed paper, or reputable journalism — not tabloid speculation or unverified pop-true-crime lore?
- **Non-Sensationalism** — does the work stay in academic/clinical register, with zero glorification, "mastermind" framing, or entertainment tone?
- **Framework Application Accuracy** — is the named framework (PCL-R, violentization stages, organized/disorganized typology) applied correctly and to the right scope, not name-dropped?
- **Epistemic Humility on Predictive Claims** — does the work correctly avoid overclaiming that any framework (warning signs, profiling, brain imaging) predicts future violence, and does it distinguish retrospective pattern-matching from prospective prediction?

## Assessment Form

A short concept-check quiz after each lesson's theory portion, plus — mirroring `domains/psychology/DOMAIN.md` — a "myth vs. fact" check before Applied Practice begins each module, testing whether a popular true-crime claim related to the lesson (e.g. "serial killers are geniuses," "you can always spot one") holds up against the documented evidence before the learner applies the real framework.

## Practitioner Reference Frame

Compare how an academic forensic psychologist (Hare-tradition trait-based assessment, peer-reviewed and cautious about predictive claims) would approach a case against how an FBI Behavioral Analysis Unit profiler (Ressler/Douglas-tradition investigative-typology approach, useful investigatively but with documented accuracy limits per Snook et al.) would approach it, against how true-crime media/pop culture would frame the same case (sensationalized, mastermind-narrative, unreliable) — making the evidence-quality gap explicit every time. Reference figures: Robert Hare, Kent Kiehl, Lonnie Athens, Robert Ressler & John Douglas (FBI profiling originators), Brent Snook & David Canter (leading academic critics of profiling validity). Reference organizations: FBI Behavioral Analysis Unit (BAU), academic forensic/criminal psychology journals (e.g. *Criminal Justice and Behavior*, *Journal of Interpersonal Violence*).

## Domain-Specific Standards

- **No armchair diagnosis rule**: never diagnose any living private individual (the learner, acquaintances, public figures without a documented forensic evaluation on record). Case studies are restricted to offenders with an extensive, closed, public court/confession record (e.g. Bundy, Rader, Dahmer) — never active/unsolved cases where speculation could name a wrong suspect.
- **Non-sensationalism rule**: all case material stays in academic/clinical framing. No "ranking" of killers, no glamorization language, no treating case studies as entertainment. Every case-study lesson opens by naming real victims with dignity, not just the offender.
- **Primary/documented-source-only rule**: every factual case claim must cite a traceable source (court record, confession transcript, peer-reviewed paper, reputable long-form journalism) — never tabloid or unverified social-media true-crime content.
- **Predictive-limits rule**: every profiling/warning-sign/brain-imaging framework must be taught alongside its documented reliability/validity limitations in the same lesson — never presented as reliably predictive of future violence.
- Otherwise, `standards/UNIVERSAL_STANDARDS.md` is sufficient — add `domains/serial-killers/standards/` only if a recurring need for domain-specific rigor rules emerges.
